The 30-day refund rule, applied the same way on every ticket.

Six made-up refund requests. Each answer shows the order-log line behind it, and edge cases go to a person.

Checking

A full refund needs three facts: the ticket came within 30 days of purchase, the QR code was never scanned, and the eSIM was never installed or activated. Installed or activated means no refund, even with no data used.

"Not scanned" needs a supplier status check from the last 24 hours. A log with no scan line is not proof. A fact with no line behind it reads "not found", and the request goes to a person. So do outages, earlier refunds and logs that disagree.
